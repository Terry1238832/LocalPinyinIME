Character context statistics
The compact context model contains 1-4 character counts derived from original project-authored prose (MIT) and public-domain original prose by Lu Xun (1881-1936) and Zhu Ziqing (1898-1948).
The full prose and Python authoring dependencies are not included in the installer.
Source titles, authors, links, rights review and checksums: https://github.com/Terry1238832/LocalPinyinIME/blob/main/resources/dictionary/authoring/sources.json
Model transformation and checksums: resources/dictionary/context_zh.metadata.json in the source repository.
Validation works are excluded from training.


Character language model (context_zh.lm)
The 4-gram language model used to rank candidates by the preceding text contains only smoothed character n-gram statistics (no sentences) estimated from:
- the project-authored and public-domain prose listed above;
- Tatoeba Mandarin sentences, https://tatoeba.org, licensed CC BY 2.0 FR (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/fr/), by the Tatoeba contributors;
- Chinese Wikinews (zh.wikinews.org) article dump of 2026-10-01, licensed CC BY 2.5 / CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.5/), by the Wikinews contributors;
- Mozilla Common Voice zh-CN and zh-TW sentence text, released under CC0 1.0 (https://github.com/common-voice/common-voice, commit 7015419eb71f82ac8121f91d3f4c5338b83d6844);
- internal n-grams of the bundled dictionary words.
Text was converted to simplified Chinese with OpenCC (Apache-2.0), which is used at authoring time only.
Sources, downloads and checksums: resources/dictionary/authoring/context_lm_sources.json and resources/dictionary/context_zh.lm.metadata.json in the source repository.
One line in forty and all evaluation sentences are held out of training.


Next-text continuations
The bundled short next-text continuation pairs are original first-party text authored by LocalPinyinIME contributors and distributed under the project MIT license.
Weights are relative editorial preferences. No corpus, commercial lexicon or user conversation history is imported.
Authorship, review and checksums: resources/dictionary/authoring/continuations_sources.json and resources/dictionary/continuations_zh.metadata.json.

game identifiers and titles, with project-generated spelling/pinyin keys and
editorial ranking weights. They contain no reference article bodies, pictures
or logos. The MIT license for project code is not a license grant for source-site
content or trademarks. Exact page/revision manifests were not supplied upstream;
do not infer unrestricted source-content redistribution or commercial permission.
